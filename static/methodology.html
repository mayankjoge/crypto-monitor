Methodology and Limitations

Back to dashboard

Data

Prices, 24h volume and market cap come from CoinGecko's public API (/coins/markets, /coins/{id}/market_chart). Responses are validated (malformed and duplicate rows dropped, missing fields shown as "n/a") and cached. If a refresh fails, cached data is shown with a visible stale warning; if none exists, an error is shown, never a fake price. In mock mode, all numbers are synthetic and a banner says so.

Indicators

  • SMA(n): mean of the last n daily closes. Lags price; says nothing about direction ahead.
  • Volatility: sample standard deviation of daily simple returns over the selected period (percent). Past variability only.
  • Volume / market cap: a rough activity ratio. Distorted by inflated or wash-traded volume.

Rankings and screening

Most actively traded = descending 24h volume. Potential opportunity = coins that satisfy all user-set thresholds on 24h change, volume/market cap and market cap. Every result shows its metrics. The screen is a filter on measurable data, not a recommendation.

Limitations

  • Forecasting and backtesting are not implemented; the site makes no predictions.
  • Free API data may be delayed, rate-limited, or differ across providers.
  • The watchlist is stored in your browser only (localStorage).
  • Educational use only; not financial advice.
